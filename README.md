# Risk and Return Analysis of Maruti Suzuki

I completed this project as a part of my college final year project for my Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) at St. Joseph’s Degree & PG College, Hyderabad.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the risk and return performance of Maruti Suzuki over a five-year period from 2020 to 2024.

The study uses historical stock price data collected from Yahoo Finance and Investing.com. Monthly returns, average rate of return, and standard deviation were calculated to evaluate the company's stock performance and volatility.

## Objectives

- Analyze Maruti Suzuki's risk and return performance.
- Calculate monthly and average returns.
- Measure stock volatility using standard deviation.
- Identify yearly variations in risk and return.
- Understand the relationship between risk and return.

## Key Findings

- 2020 recorded the highest volatility with a standard deviation of approximately 12.91%.
- 2021 recorded a slightly negative average return.
- 2022 showed signs of recovery.
- 2023 recorded the highest average return of approximately 1.80% with the lowest risk of around 4.01%.
- 2024 recorded positive returns but with increased volatility.

## Tools & Methods

- Historical Stock Price Data
- Monthly Return Calculation
- Average Rate of Return
- Standard Deviation
- Data Analysis
- Tables, Graphs and Charts

## Conclusion

The analysis showed that Maruti Suzuki's risk and return varied significantly between 2020 and 2024. The relationship between risk and return was not consistent across all years, highlighting the impact of changing market and economic conditions.
